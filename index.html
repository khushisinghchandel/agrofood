FROM THE FIELD TO YOUR PLATE

History of Paddy Rice in Madhya Pradesh

Madhya Pradesh has a diverse rice-growing landscape with cultivation spread across many districts. The region's varied agricultural conditions support a wide range of paddy varieties and farming traditions.

At Arna Agro Food, we build on that agricultural heritage by combining careful sourcing with modern processing and quality control. Our goal is simple: consistent grains that make everyday cooking feel special.
